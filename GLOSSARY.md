# Course Platform

An online platform where learners buy and take video courses.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to hand back a purchased course and get their money back, with their reason. It is Requested, then Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner when their Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The period after a purchase during which a learner may raise a Refund Request.

**Chargeback**:
A card dispute the cardholder raises with their bank, forced on the platform from outside. It is not a Refund Request.
