# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase, and what that does to a learner's access.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. The team approves or declines it, or the learner withdraws it first. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a purchase may still raise a Refund Request: within 14 days of buying, under 2 hours of video watched, under half the lessons completed, and not a claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: Refund window (for the whole rule; the window is only the 14 days)

**Exception Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an admin raises for a learner, with a justification, on a purchase that lacks Refund Eligibility. A different admin decides it.
_Avoid_: Override, Manual refund, Goodwill refund

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team hasn't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team has turned down. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Withdrawn Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the learner took back before the team decided it. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request while still eligible.
_Avoid_: Cancelled, Retracted, Declined (for a learner's own change of mind)

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund. A learner gets at most one Refund per course, even if they buy it again, unless it comes from an Exception Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund the team has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Finance**:
The duty of sending Refunds and confirming whether the money went out. It is held alongside someone's usual role, not instead of it, and is separate from deciding Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Financial controller, Accountant, Billing

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.

### Enrollments

**Enrollment**:
A learner's access to a course, gained by buying it or claiming a team seat. A learner can only view a course's content while they have an active Enrollment.
_Avoid_: Subscription, Membership, Access (for the record itself)

**Ended Enrollment**:
An Enrollment that no longer gives access, because its purchase was refunded. It stays on record, and the learner's progress is kept for if they buy again, which starts a new Enrollment.
_Avoid_: Deleted, Revoked, Unenrolled
